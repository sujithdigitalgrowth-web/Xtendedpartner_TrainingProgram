Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Xtended Partner ("we," "us") collects from you when you interact with the Job-Ready Program website and enrollment process, and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you message us on WhatsApp, email us, or reserve a seat. This may include your name, phone number, email address, and any details you choose to share about your background or goals.

If analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel) are active on this site, we may also collect standard usage data — pages visited, device/browser type, and referral source — to understand how visitors use the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and confirm seat reservations
  • To communicate program details, schedules, and updates via WhatsApp, email, or phone
  • To improve the program and this website based on aggregate usage patterns

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the program (for example, WhatsApp Business for communication, or a payment processor to collect fees), or where required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to communicate with you about the program and to meet reasonable business and legal record-keeping needs. You can request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Choices

You can ask us to correct, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to the email address below. You can also unsubscribe from WhatsApp or email communication at any time by telling us directly.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@xtendedpartner.com.
